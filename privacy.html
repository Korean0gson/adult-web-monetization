Privacy Policy.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy describes how 0kbanana.com handles information when you visit this website. The site includes affiliate links, advertising, and optional third-party features. Those third parties may process information under their own privacy policies.

Information and browser storage

The site stores your optional-feature choices in your browser's local storage so it can remember them on later visits. The site also uses essential browser storage to keep these preferences. Banner images are loaded after you confirm your age; they are not controlled by the optional-feature settings.

Optional analytics and third-party features

Optional features are off unless you allow them in Cookie settings. If enabled, Cloudflare Web Analytics, the CrakRevenue cam feed, the sponsored player offer, the GoLove.ai chat head, or Google IMA for VAST ads may load third-party code or content. These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device details, referring page, and interactions, and may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own practices.

The IMA script is only loaded when VAST ads are allowed and ad-tag and video sources have been configured. No VAST sources are currently configured.

Affiliate links and advertising

Some links are affiliate links. If you follow them, the linked provider may receive referral or tracking information and may set its own cookies or similar technologies. We may earn a commission if you visit or purchase through an affiliate link, at no additional cost to you. Sponsored content is provided by third parties and is subject to their privacy terms.

Your choices

You can open Cookie settings from the bottom notice or the site footer to allow or reject each optional feature. You can also clear this site's local storage through your browser settings. Changing or withdrawing a choice stops future loading where possible; a page reload may be needed to remove interface elements already added by a third-party script.

Children and age confirmation

This website is intended for adults only. Its age screen is a self-attestation prompt, not identity or legal age verification. Do not use the site if you are under 18 or below the age of majority where you live.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when site features or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact okenterprises@currently.com. Review this policy against applicable legal requirements before relying on it for legal or regulatory purposes.
